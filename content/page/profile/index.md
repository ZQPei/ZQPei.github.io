## Biography

I obtained my master's degree at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2020, advised by Prof. Guoyou Wang. Before that, I obtained my Bachelor of Science degree from South West University in 2013. I then worked as a high school physics teacher for four years until 2017.

I am broadly interested in Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and AI Infrastructure. My focus areas include Deep Learning Compiler Stack and CUDA optimization.
